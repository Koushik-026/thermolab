THE MATHEMATICS, MADE HUMAN

A small equation.
A familiar kind of change.

The bigger the temperature difference, the faster the change. As the gap gets smaller, the curve begins to settle.

dT / dt = −k(T − Troom)

T is the object temperature. t is time in minutes. k tells us how quickly the gap shrinks.

01

The exact solution

The equation gives the temperature directly at any time. This is our reference.

T(t) = Troom + (T₀ − Troom)e−kt
02

Euler’s small steps

Use the current rate to estimate the next temperature. Smaller steps usually give a closer result.

Next temperature = current + step × rate
03

Runge–Kutta, a closer look

Sample four rates inside each step and combine them for a more accurate estimate at a suitable step size.
